# Voice Samples

John adds to this file over time. Read it when drafting, alongside the kill list, to calibrate voice. These excerpts show how he writes: examples to learn rhythm from, not text to copy. They are not facts to reuse in a new piece.

## Should You Still Hire Junior Developers?

Source: [Should You Still Hire Junior Developers?](https://developkerr.com/blog/should-you-still-hire-junior-developers/) on developkerr.com. Closing section; John's own edit.

> When I started out I was working on a big website but doing simple front-end tasks. I had to wait two years and change jobs before I got to touch back-end logic. Juniors can now build whole projects in their lunchtime. That's hugely empowering but also raises the bar on the number of things they need to hold in their head.
>
> The game has completely changed, but that is nothing new. As those front-end skills I spent two years refining now atrophy, my advice is clear - if you want to have a long career in software engineering get comfortable with being uncomfortable.

From the same post (John's edit):

> Most of engineering is maintenance and dealing with issues you couldn't anticipate without real usage. ... Bad decisions compound over time and you need to have enough awareness to course correct when that starts to happen.
